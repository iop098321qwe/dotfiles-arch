# Grillme Question Bank

Use this as a checklist for coverage, not as a script. Ask only questions that
are relevant to the user's actual request. Convert each category into specific
questions tied to the project, task, decision, or deliverable at hand.

## How to Use This Reference

- Start with categories whose answers could change the outcome.
- Skip categories that cannot affect scope, quality, risk, validation, or
  delivery.
- Revisit categories after each answer because new risks can appear.
- Prefer concrete, context-specific questions over generic templates.
- Record `skip`, `I don't know`, and delegated choices exactly.

## Goal and Outcome

- What problem should this solve?
- What result would make the work clearly successful?
- What should be different after the work is complete?
- What is the priority if goals conflict?
- What should not be optimized for?

## Scope and Boundaries

- What is explicitly in scope?
- What is explicitly out of scope?
- What existing behavior, process, or output must remain unchanged?
- What can be deferred without making the result incorrect?
- What would count as scope creep?

## Users and Stakeholders

- Who will use, review, approve, maintain, or be affected by this?
- What do those people need that may not be obvious from the request?
- Are there conflicting stakeholder priorities?
- Who can make final decisions when tradeoffs appear?

## Current State

- What exists today?
- What is broken, missing, inefficient, or unclear now?
- What prior attempts, decisions, or constraints should be preserved?
- What context is available in files, tools, history, or examples?

## Inputs and Sources

- What inputs, materials, data, examples, or references should be used?
- Which sources are authoritative when they conflict?
- What inputs may be missing, malformed, delayed, or unreliable?
- What assumptions about input shape, timing, volume, or quality matter?

## Outputs and Deliverables

- What exact outputs should be produced?
- What format, structure, location, or interface is required?
- What level of detail is expected?
- What examples of acceptable and unacceptable output exist?
- How should partial, empty, failed, or ambiguous results be handled?

## Behavior and Rules

- What rules must the solution follow?
- What edge cases could change the expected behavior?
- What errors or exceptional states must be handled?
- What should happen by default when information is missing?
- What should be configurable, fixed, optional, or automatic?

## Constraints and Preferences

- What constraints are fixed by policy, tooling, platform, budget, time, or
  compatibility?
- What preferences are flexible if a better method exists?
- What naming, style, workflow, or design conventions must be followed?
- What dependencies, integrations, or services are allowed or forbidden?

## Risks and Failure Modes

- Where is the request ambiguous, fragile, or likely to fail?
- What could create regressions, delays, rework, or user confusion?
- What security, privacy, compliance, safety, or operational risks exist?
- What hidden coupling or downstream impact might matter?
- What should be monitored, reviewed, or verified afterward?

## Tradeoffs and Better Methods

- Is there a simpler way to achieve the same goal?
- Is the requested approach solving the root problem or a symptom?
- What alternatives reduce risk, cost, complexity, or maintenance burden?
- What would be lost by choosing the better alternative?
- What decision needs explicit user approval?

## Acceptance and Validation

- What must be true before the work is considered done?
- How will correctness be tested, reviewed, demonstrated, or measured?
- What manual checks are required when automation is unavailable?
- What evidence should be reported back to the user?
- What residual risks should remain visible after completion?

## Delivery and Follow-Through

- Should the work be done incrementally or in one pass?
- What order of operations reduces risk?
- What should be committed, shipped, documented, or handed off?
- What should happen if a blocker or contradiction appears?
- What future maintenance or ownership expectations exist?
